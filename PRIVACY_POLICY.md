# Privacy Policy for ShiftSync

**Last updated:** September 2026

ShiftSync ("the app") is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app uses and, more importantly, what it does not do with it.

## Data We Collect

**None.** ShiftSync does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data to us or any third party. There are no servers, no accounts, and no analytics or advertising SDKs in this app.

## Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores the following information locally, using Apple's standard on-device storage (`UserDefaults`):

- Shift entries you log (start/end times, shift type, notes)
- Your display name, email, job title, and branch (optional, entered by you)
- Pay settings (currency, hourly/monthly rate, work day hours, overtime rules)
- Your workplace location (latitude/longitude), if you choose to set one for arrival/departure alerts
- App preferences (theme, vacation day allowance)

This data never leaves your device except:
- As part of a standard encrypted iOS device backup (iCloud or Finder/computer backup), which is controlled entirely by your iOS backup settings — not by this app.
- When you explicitly choose to export a CSV or PDF report and share it yourself (e.g. via AirDrop, email, or Files).

## Location Data

If you enable Workplace Geofencing, ShiftSync uses your device's location — including in the background — solely to detect when you arrive at or leave the workplace you set, so it can remind you to clock in or out. Location data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Notifications

ShiftSync sends local notifications (clock in/out confirmations, arrival/departure alerts, missed-shift reminders) generated entirely on your device. If you have a paired Apple Watch, these notifications may mirror to your watch via Apple's standard WatchConnectivity framework — this stays between your own devices and does not involve any external server.

## Children's Privacy

ShiftSync does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Since the app collects no data, changes are expected to be rare and minor.
